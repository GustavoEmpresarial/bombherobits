The collection is presented in the public /auth page after the original three heroes. These are character designs with presentation rarities; no new actors were added to the playable summon pool, no odds were changed and existing heroes were preserved. Blue, violet, purple, gold and red card treatments follow the supplied visual reference.

All eight originals were verified as RGBA with alpha spanning 0–255. Browser verification confirmed exactly eight loaded portraits, the expected rarity distribution, intact full-body art, and no horizontal overflow at 1280px or 375px. The full collection preview is saved as `apps/web/public/characters/collection/collection-preview.png`.
